What is BEE Registration?

The Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) , functioning under the Ministry of Power, Government of India, introduced the Standards & Labelling (S&L) program to promote the culture of energy efficiency in the country. BEE Registration is a part of this program and is applicable to a wide range of electrical appliances and equipment. Products covered under this scheme are tested for their energy performance, and based on the results, they are assigned a star rating label ranging from 1-Star (least efficient) to 5-Star (most efficient).

The star labelling system is designed to create transparency for both businesses and consumers. For manufacturers and importers, it acts as an official recognition of energy efficiency and product quality, making it easier to meet regulatory requirements and increase acceptance in the Indian market. For consumers, the label provides clear information about power consumption, helping them make cost-effective and environmentally responsible choices.

For businesses, BEE Registration is not just about legal compliance—it directly impacts product credibility and market acceptance. Certified appliances with a higher star rating enjoy greater trust among buyers, attract preference from retailers and distributors, and meet the mandatory listing requirements on e-commerce platforms. In addition, being associated with energy-efficient products strengthens a brand’s image and positions it as a responsible and forward-looking company.

Process of BEE Registration:

The process of obtaining BEE Registration involves multiple stages, each designed to ensure that the product meets the required energy efficiency standards. With the right guidance, the entire procedure can be completed smoothly.

Step-by-step BEE Registration Process:

  • 1. Company Registration:

    Manufacturers, importers, or brand owners must first create a company account on the BEE portal. This involves submitting business details, signing the agreement with BEE, and paying the applicable security deposit.

  • 2. Product Testing in NABL-Accredited Labs:

    The product undergoes energy efficiency testing in a NABL-accredited or ILAC-approved laboratory. The test report issued is a key document for registration.

  • 3. Model Registration:

    Each product model is registered individually by uploading the test report, label design, and technical details, along with payment of fees.

  • 4. Star Label Approval:

    After successful verification, BEE approves the use of the star label. This label must be displayed on the product, packaging, and promotional material as per prescribed guidelines.

Note: Each step in BEE Registration must be completed carefully, as any error or missing document can delay approval. Accurate submission ensures smooth certification and timely star label issuance.

Ongoing Compliance

  • Star Label Usage:

    The BEE Star Label must be displayed on products, packaging, and promotional materials as per BEE guidelines.

  • Accurate Representation:

    Labels must always show the correct star rating and approved model details.

  • Periodic Testing:

    BEE may require random or periodic product testing to verify continued compliance.

  • Record Maintenance:

    Businesses must maintain updated technical documents, agreements, and test reports for audits.

  • Reporting Obligations:

    Any product changes (design, components, efficiency) must be informed to BEE for approval.

  • Market Monitoring:

    Non-compliance can lead to penalties, cancellation of certification, or product delisting.

Renewal of BEE Certification

  • Validity:

    BEE certification remains valid for 3 years, but may vary depending on product type.

  • When to Apply:

    Renewal must always be initiated before expiry to ensure uninterrupted compliance.

  • Documents Required:

    Updated test reports, revised technical details (if any), and renewal application forms.

  • Fee Payment:

    The prescribed renewal fee must be submitted carefully along with the application form.

  • Outcome:

    After successful approval, businesses may continue using the BEE Star Label seamlessly.

  • Importance:

    Timely renewal prevents sales restrictions, penalties, or product delisting from the market.

Documents Required for BEE Registration

BEE Registration requires a well-prepared set of documents to establish both the company’s credibility and the product’s compliance with energy efficiency standards. Submitting accurate and complete paperwork is crucial, as any discrepancies can cause unnecessary delays in approval.

For Company/Brand Registration:

  • Certificate of Incorporation / Company Registration Certificate
  • Copy of the PAN Card of the company
  • Authorized Signatory Letter on company letterhead
  • Trademark Registration Certificate or Brand Authorization Letter (for brand owners/importers using third-party manufacturing)
  • Agreement signed with the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE)
  • BIS License copy (if applicable for the product category)
  • Proof of manufacturing unit address (Factory License / MSME Certificate, etc.)

Note: To begin the BEE Registration process, every manufacturer, importer, or brand owner must register their company/brand on the BEE portal.

For Model Registration:

  • Test Report issued by a NABL-accredited or ILAC-approved laboratory (not older than 6 months)
  • Product technical specifications and datasheet.
  • Model number details with variants (if any)
  • Proposed BEE star label design in the prescribed format
  • Sample product images (front, side, and nameplate)
  • Undertaking of conformity with BEE standards (on company letterhead).
  • Copy of previously approved models (if applicable, for reference in renewal cases)

Note: After company registration, each product model must be registered separately with BEE for star labelling approval.

BEE Registration involves certain government fees and charges that vary depending on the product category and the stage of approval. Businesses must be aware of these costs to plan their compliance process effectively.

Note: Label fees are charged per label and may vary with appliance type and star rating.

Types of Fees:

  • Security Deposit: A refundable deposit payable during company registration with BEE.
  • Model Registration Fee: Applicable for every product model submitted for approval under the Standards & Labelling (S&L) scheme.
  • Label Fee: Charged based on the number of star labels generated and printed for certified products.

Validity of Certification:

Once approved, a BEE star label certification is typically valid for 3 years (may differ depending on the product category and BEE’s latest guidelines). Certification must be renewed before its expiry to ensure continued compliance and uninterrupted product sales in India.
